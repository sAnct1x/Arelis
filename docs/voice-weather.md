# Weather out loud

When you ask for the weather, she answers in a spoken sentence: how
warm it is, what the sky looks like, and the next few days. Chat and
voice use that same sentence.

The tool still writes a short fact dump for the model (place, then a
colon, then the numbers). That dump is not what you hear or see. If the
model leaves the bubble empty, or pastes the dump back, she still
ships the spoken sentence.

Degrees are said as "degrees Fahrenheit" (or Celsius when that is what
the line uses).
