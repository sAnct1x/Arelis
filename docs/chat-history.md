# Chat history

Every conversation is saved as you go, including after a restart. Open
**History** (View → History, or Ctrl+Shift+F) to find an earlier chat and
click it to bring the whole conversation back. You can keep talking from
there.

Starting Arelis opens a new chat. Yesterday's thread stays in History; it
is not loaded automatically.

## Search

The box at the top of History looks through titles and the text of
messages, not just the names of recent chats. Type a word you remember
from the conversation. Ctrl+Shift+F focuses that box. (Ctrl+F finds text
inside the chat you already have open.)

## If something stops mid-reply

If Arelis is stopped, or the app closes, while she is still writing, the
words already on screen are kept. Reopen that chat from History to see
them and continue.

## Pin, delete, new

Right-click a chat to pin it to the top of the list, or to delete it.
Delete cannot be undone. The **new** button (or Ctrl+N) starts a fresh
conversation.
