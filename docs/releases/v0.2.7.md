# Arelis v0.2.7

What the 0.2.7 installer ships. Living notes for a source checkout are
in [whats-new.md](../whats-new.md).

0.2.6 finished the conversation turns. That ship still had holes on
the live board: a screenshot could fire SMS, a file on the drop tray
was unread, a failed browser drive could latch the next ask, and a
new chapter reused last night's thread.

This is the installer that closes those.

---

## Glass

OCR does not send a text. A file you drop on the tray is readable.
A failed drive does not stay latched onto the next ask. A new
chapter opens a new chat; yesterday stays in History.

Turn routing, leftover-recipient notices, and no-call / wander
paths live in named tables. The behavior is the same. The copies
in the orchestrator mixins are gone.

## Still true from 0.2.6

Deep research is 32 rounds even on the default fast chip. Empty
chat after a scrape asks her to write. A granted folder includes
the files inside it. The prefix cache, skinny schemas, Reality as
a permanent room (slash id `physics`), her Chrome on one desk.
The 3D plate, hands, and the Earth zone stay source-checkout. The
installer is still not code-signed. Ollama and the voice weights
are still not inside the setup `.exe`.
