# Arelis v0.2.7

What you get if you install this one. Checkout notes stay in
[whats-new.md](../whats-new.md).

0.2.6 made her finish a research turn. The next four days were the
rest of the house — pictures, the desk, mail, texts, calendar, and
the leftover ways she still did the wrong job. This is that tree.

---

## She does the thing you asked

Each ask gets a one-line goal. A temperature spec is not weather.
"1960–2026" is not subtraction. "email it to me" is you, not a
contact named it. A finished SMS that still says "I love her"
does not go out. A Bloomberg captcha is not the research report.
Algebra stays in chat — she does not open a 32-round crawl and a
PDF for that.

A screenshot is not a text. Drop a file on the tray and she can
read it. If her browser trip dies, the next thing you ask is a
new ask. Start a new chapter and you get a new chat. Last night
stays in History.

## Pictures

Generation is Comfy: square / 16:9 / portrait, a style, four
versions if you ask. Hand her a photo and "make this a watercolor"
edits that photo. It does not invent a new scene. Cut-out and
outpaint stay on `image`. Resize, crop, upscale, rotate, flip
stay on `image_edit`.

The desk has a picture well — file name, prompt, square thumbs,
left / right. A pasted photo plus "who is this?" may name a
public figure. The live camera still will not name who is in
the room.

## Desk

The workspace does not pop open empty every time she researches.
A PDF or report she just wrote lands on the desk and opens.
Opening a file no longer leaves a vacant brown column beside
the page.

TeX in a note (`$$`, `\frac`) becomes readable math in the file
and in the chat bubble.

## Allow

Pictures, files, seeing, and her window are the grant. Mail,
texts, deletes, Pay, and programs still show the card. Settings
splits that mode from the class boxes: uncheck a class to never
ask. Don't ask again turns that class off.

## Notifications, texts, mail, calendar

The inbox is an unread pile. Click a row and that thing opens —
a text becomes the chat tile, a job opens the file, mail opens
a reader you can reply from. Snooze is 5 / 15 / 60 minutes or
tomorrow. Re-indexing the phone does not dump last week's texts
as new.

Text threads survive a restart. Bubbles have a time. A failed
send gets retry. Contacts have a Text action.

Mail and the phone token go in Settings → notify. You do not
edit `secrets.yaml` for that. A job that needs mail says so
when mail is missing.

Calendar works locally without Google. If the Google token dies
she still writes the event, then opens a sign-in — no terminal
command. Same title and time is one event, not two. A month
cell with too many events says **+N more**. A standing auth
miss is a **sign in** chip, not a modal.

## Her Chrome

Tell her the errand. If you grab the mouse in her window she
freezes instead of fighting you. Sign-in on X is your turn —
she does not keep clicking Continue with phone. A click timeout
does not drop the tab.

## Phone

First open of the day is a new chat, same as the desktop.
Yesterday stays in chats.

## Reality (source checkout)

The 3D plate, hands, and Earth still need a source checkout.
Reality as a room still ships.

Earth is a zone you enter after you travel there. Say take me
to Tokyo — or a street address — and she flies there. The chips
follow the dest, not the leftover travel standoff. Double-click
a plane or a quake and you go to it. Marks look like what they
are: a plane, a ship, the station — not a chevron and a ring.
Streets are named roads on the planet, with a compass and a
distance meter. Enter turns Live on. Leave and the solar lab
comes back.

## Same as 0.2.6

"Deeply research" still gets the long run even if the chip says
fast. After a long scrape she writes in her own words instead of
pasting the page. Allow a folder and she can see the files
inside it.

Still not signed. Ollama and the voice weights are still not in
the `.exe`.
