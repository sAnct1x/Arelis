# Arelis v0.3.4

Arelis is a locally hosted personal AI assistant, with a focus on science and research. This release is mostly about Filament: Arelis now stands in the room with an orbit tree around her, startup is cleaner, and the webcam stays off until you turn it on.

- Filament is the main theme and the default for new installs. Sodium and Night are still there as Sodium (legacy) and Night (legacy), and a saved choice is kept. Filament wears Arelis's own violet. ([#520](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/520), [#523](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/523), [#525](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/525))
- In Filament, Arelis stands full height beside a tree that grows from her, and the tiles you open grow out of its branches. She stands a little smaller than before, feet on the floor line. Tiles keep the size you give them when you drag them. ([#520](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/520), [#523](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/523), [#526](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/526))
- Her figure moves more naturally: she stands upright, rests her head a little toward the chat, keeps her head steady after a slow frame, and her lips move while she types a reply. ([#516](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/516), [#517](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/517), [#518](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/518), [#519](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/519), [#521](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/521), [#522](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/522))
- Arelis now uses the new Quick 3D figure by default, and the installer ships her model. Set `ARELIS_AVATAR_ENGINE=web` for the old figure. ([#484](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/484), [#504](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/504), [#505](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/505))
- Startup: a splash shows what is loading and stays up until the app is fully drawn, then the window appears in one step. If something takes more than 5 minutes, the app opens anyway. ([#523](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/523), [#530](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/530))
- Privacy: the webcam never turns on by itself. The camera window opens with the camera off and a Start camera button, and Hands starts off every launch. ([#526](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/526), [#527](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/527))
- The first reply after Arelis starts is faster, because the startup warm-up now really works. ([#483](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/483))
- Fixes: Night and Sodium no longer freeze or open black on a maximized start, clicking a chat in History opens it, the avatar model is found in the installed app folder from a source checkout, a picture is found by its bare name in a workspace subfolder, and data-file questions read the active workspace folder again. ([#512](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/512), [#513](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/513), [#528](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/528), [#477](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/477), [#475](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/475), [#457](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/457), [#459](https://github.com/sAnct1x/Arelis/pull/459))

Installer still isn't signed, so Windows will warn you. Hit More info, then Run anyway.
