# Arelis v0.2.4

What the 0.2.4 installer ships. Living notes for a source checkout are
in [whats-new.md](../whats-new.md).

The glass is quieter. Turns keep the tool schemas so Ollama can reuse
the prefix. Reality is a permanent room. The 3D plate and the Earth
zone stay on a source checkout — they are not in this setup `.exe`.

---

## Turns

Every turn carries the full tool schema list so Ollama can reuse the
prefix. A greeting that skipped schemas (the 0.2.3 clock-ask shortcut)
blew that cache and made the next real question pay tens of seconds of
prefill.

Tiny asks (clock, hello, thanks, who are you) still stream. Tool-bearing
turns still hold the answer until the tools finish. "Who is this" is
not identity.

Launch pins the model, then seeds that prefix. The window says
**loading the model…**, not **thinking…**, until the seed is done.

## Glass

One lowercase voice. Settings tabs are `audio` / `window` / `allow` /
`notify` / `roots` / `memory`. Idle readout is `ollama` / `listening`.
The strip's menu is **house**. An empty history title is **new chat**.
Hue stays sodium (`#ff7a22`). Icons stay unlabeled.

Thinking is the essay. Housekeeping sits under it as a footer. After
the last finished answer: `copy` · `again`. Workspace is file names,
one chrome row, `..` to go up.

## Reality

The room (slash id `physics`) cannot be forgotten. Installed copies
get the room: chat, CAS, Horizons. The true-scale plate, hands, and
the Earth zone need a source checkout and the astro extra. Earth is a
zone inside Reality, not a second room.

## Not in this installer

- The 3D Reality plate and the Earth zone
- Code signing (SmartScreen will still warn)
- Bundling Ollama inside the setup `.exe`
