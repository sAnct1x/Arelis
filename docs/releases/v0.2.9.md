# Arelis v0.2.9

What you get if you install this one. Checkout notes stay in
[whats-new.md](../whats-new.md).

0.2.8 was the daily-driver pass. This one is the live-test pass:
weather for *here*, forget that actually forgets, a file read that
stays the file, and the ear that was dropping doorbells.

---

## She does the thing you asked

Tomorrow's weather with no city named uses your profile location.
She does not geocode the profile city (first gazetteer hit) or treat
"to morrow" as a place. Two named cities still get two readings.

Forget drops the facts *and* episodes you listed. Compact stamps
like `20260810-011327` are not subtraction, and "2–3 hours" in a
story is not a calculator turn.

A named file she read is the file. A 5 KB `index.html` is not a
scrape card of title tags. `workspace grep` / `find` were already
there; she needs them when she does not have the path.

Listing a folder outside the current project opens that folder in
Folders — it does not stay on the Arelis tree.

## Voice

A bare **Hey Arelis** latches Talk and stays on listening until
you say the question. Close-to-tray keeps the ear. Wake starts on
Sherpa instead of waiting out a HuggingFace Whisper check. Messy
transcripts drop ah/um and borrow names she just said. A spoken
**what did you say** replays the last answer — no new turn.
[voice-wake.md](../voice-wake.md).

## Desk

A screenshot of a monitor is a grab (`screenshot`), not named-control
`snapshot`. If named controls are missing, snapshot grabs the screen
instead of lecturing a pip install. [desktop-control.md](../desktop-control.md).
