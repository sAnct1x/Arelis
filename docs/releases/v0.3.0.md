# Arelis v0.3.0

What you get if you install this one. Checkout notes stay in
[whats-new.md](../whats-new.md).

This one is mostly fixes and safety. I am a solo student, so the
first build goes out as a release candidate, and the real 0.3.0 only
after I have tried it on a second PC.

---

## Fixed

- Unit and math questions, text extraction from Word files, and
  "open this page and tell me what it says" pick the right tool more
  often.
- Converting a file she just wrote to another format works.
- Charts accept `x` and `y` names, and the "ran out of steps" message
  says which tool failed.
- A long research task that is still reading pages is wrapped up from
  what she has found instead of being cut off.
- A reservation search for a party of 0 now means one guest, not two.
- Your paired phone can be offered a newer phone app from your PC and
  update in one tap. (Per the docs; not re-tested for this release.)

## Privacy and safety

- Your city, postal code and coordinates stay out of what the model
  sees and out of the logs by default.
- She refuses file paths that point at network shares or Windows
  device names, so a bad web page cannot make Windows start a network
  login.
- Sending mail or a text, and deleting, always pauses for your OK,
  whatever the confirm settings say. The Filament (testing) look is
  exempt.
- Before an upgrade she copies your memory and a few settings files
  (rooms, jobs, contacts, profile, lessons, local settings) into a
  dated folder under `backups`. Never your passwords or tokens. If the
  copy fails she says so before the installer starts. See
  [backups.md](../backups.md).
- When a site shows a "prove you are human" wall, `scrape` and
  `web_fetch` stop and offer to open the page in her Chrome for you.
  If you are away from Arelis for two minutes while she waits on you,
  a Windows notification says so. Nothing leaves your PC, and she does
  not try to solve the wall.
- Setup problems on first run now say what happened in plain words
  (no internet, disk full, installer did not finish, engine did not
  start) instead of a raw error string. The raw text is still in the
  log.
- Earth: removed the optional camera-search layer. Cameras still come
  from public operator catalogs, OpenStreetMap and your own cameras;
  cameras found by searching open ports stay off. If you had saved a
  key for it, it is simply ignored.
- New dark Night color option (View menu). The default look is
  unchanged.
- SECURITY.md says I aim to reply to security reports within 7 days.

## Changed, and you may notice

- Her Chrome keeps your sign-ins between runs again (a setting starts
  her window empty every run). This leaves a persistent, possibly
  signed-in profile on disk, in her own Chrome profile folder.
- A switch to turn off the once-a-day update check: put
  `updates.check: false` in your local settings file
  (`config.local.yaml` in the data folder). Off means the daily check sends
  no request to GitHub.

## Still true

- The installer is not signed, so Windows will warn you the first
  time (More info, then Run anyway). Check the hash against the
  `.sha256` file.
- Ollama and the voice files download on first run, not inside the
  installer.
- Earth and the 3D views still need a source checkout.
- An experimental setting for letting the model pick its own tools
  exists but is off, and I am not working on it.
