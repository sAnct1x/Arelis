# Saying no to one step

When you ask for several things in one message, such as saving two
files, Arelis may show a card for each step. Choosing Skip on a card
skips only that step.

Arelis then moves on to the other steps you asked for, and each one
still gets its own card. Saying no to `notes.txt` does not say no to
`todo.txt`.

If a step comes back that you already said no to in the same message,
Arelis skips it without a new card and ends the turn with a short line
naming what she did not do.
