# Her figure in Night and Sodium

In the Night and Sodium rooms she stands in a band on the right side of the
window, head to toe. When the window gets smaller she scales down to fit. She
is never cropped.

She stays whole in every pose: resting, listening, thinking, speaking and done.
Before, in Sodium, she could be cut off at the chest while listening, smiling
or talking.

## Speaking while a reply comes in

She takes her speaking pose as soon as the first words of a reply appear, and
holds it while the rest of the reply streams in. When the reply is finished
she moves to done. If spoken replies are on, she keeps speaking until the voice
stops.
