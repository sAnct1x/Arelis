# Legacy themes: the idle orbit and Arelis

Filament is the main theme and the default for new installs. Sodium and night
are listed in View → themes as **Sodium (legacy)** and **Night (legacy)**. They
still fully work. If you already picked sodium or night, you keep it. An
install that was set up before filament became the default, and never picked a
theme, stays on sodium.

## Arelis in sodium and night

Her 3D figure shows in her panel in every theme. In sodium and night she wears
the room's colour: amber on sodium, her own violet on night. Only the colour
changes. Her shape, face and motion are the same as in filament. Switching
theme retints her straight away. When the 3D view cannot run, the painted face
is used, as before.

## The idle orbit

When no conversation is open, sodium and night show an orbit in the middle of
the window in her aether look: a tall frame of fine threads, rings that ripple
slowly outward across the floor, twinkling stars, and a spark that travels the
frame. Every glow and particle moves; nothing in it is still. It takes the
same colour as Arelis in that room. The smaller orbit beside the composer, and
the orb in her panel while her face loads, use the same look.

It breathes while she is thinking, and it stops moving when the window is
hidden.
